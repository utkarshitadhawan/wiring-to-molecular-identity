with acetylcholine and GABA as the examined neurotransmitter classes.

FIGURE 3
Principal-component representation of connectivity architecture across
the examined olfactory neurons. PC1 and PC2 explain 68.02% and 14.48%
of the total variance, respectively, with the first two components
together explaining 82.50%. The PCA provides a descriptive
representation of connectivity variation and does not establish
causality.

FIGURE 4
Performance of models evaluated using precomputed cell-type-grouped
five-fold cross-validation. Bars represent mean balanced accuracy
across validation folds and error bars represent the standard
deviation across folds. Canonical cell type was used as the grouping
variable to prevent neurons belonging to the same cell type from
being distributed between training and validation folds.

FIGURE 5
Permutation-based predictive contribution of individual connectivity
features under cell-type-grouped validation. Bars represent mean
permutation importance across validation folds and error bars
represent the corresponding standard deviation. Permutation
importance reflects predictive contribution within the evaluated
model and should not be interpreted as an independent causal effect.

FIGURE 6
Relative permutation contribution of connectivity feature families.
The contributions were calculated from the precomputed feature-level
permutation importance results and summarize the relative contribution
of different connectivity dimensions to prediction within the examined
olfactory neuron population.
